01 / AboutGet to know the cook behind the table

It started
around a table.

Pull up a chair.

I learned to cook at home, from parents who were natural hosts and had me serving drinks and talking to guests before I could reach the counter.

Since then I’ve cooked in a restaurant in Madagascar, hosted my own supper club in London, and spent my summers as a private chef in Cap Ferret.

The food matters, but what I really care about is what happens between the people at the table.

02 / What I believeThe ingredients you don't see

More than
a meal.

Three things I bring to every table.

01

Care.

Every dish is cooked with love and attention to detail. I want food that tastes generous and also leaves you feeling good, with fresh, wholesome ingredients that look after you from the inside.

02

Openness.

I seat people next to someone new. The quiet ones often end up the most talkative person at the table.

03

Belonging.

At my first supper club, ten of sixteen guests were strangers. By dessert, they were asking when the next one was.

There's always room for one more.
