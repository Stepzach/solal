About

I learned to cook at home, from parents who were natural hosts and had me serving drinks and talking to guests before I could reach the counter.

Since then I’ve cooked in a restaurant in Madagascar, hosted my own supper club in London, and spent my summers as a private chef in Cap Ferret.

The food matters, but what I really care about is what happens between the people at the table.

What I believe

  • Care. Every dish is cooked with love and attention to detail. I want food that tastes generous and also leaves you feeling good, with fresh, wholesome ingredients that look after you from the inside.
  • Openness. I seat people next to someone new. The quiet ones often end up the most talkative person at the table.
  • Belonging. At my first supper club, ten of sixteen guests were strangers. By dessert, they were asking when the next one was.

What I do

Private dinners

Generous, seasonal food cooked in your home, for 8 to 50 guests.

Supper clubs

Themed evenings where strangers share a table and leave as a group.

Events & brand catering

Relaxed, well-made food for shoots, launches and long working days, with the setting treated as part of the meal.

How it works

  • Before: we talk about your guests, their dietary needs and the feeling you want in the room.
  • During: I cook, serve and quietly keep the table connected.
  • After: your guests leave fed, and a little closer than when they arrived.

All you need to do is bring your people.
